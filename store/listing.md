## Full description (4000 max)

Mirrorlock is a calm escape puzzle with a twist you can see at a glance: every piece has a mirror twin across a glowing line, and tapping either one moves both.

Tap a piece and it slides straight off the board, and so does its twin on the other side. If anything stands in either twin's way, both bump back and you lose a heart. Twins heading into each other across the mirror always crash. Read the board, find the pair that's free, and clear every piece. Do it with no bumps for three stars.

NINE MIRRORS, ONE IDEA AT A TIME
Each chapter adds one new idea and opens with a room that teaches it:
• Reflect: a mirror down the middle, left and right swap
• Horizon: the mirror lies down, up and down swap
• Stones: a stone on one side only breaks the symmetry, and crumbles as you play
• Solo: lonely pieces with no twin move alone
• Slant: a mirror from corner to corner turns every twin a quarter turn
• Vault: a locked pair waits until its key pair escapes
• Kaleido: two mirrors at once, four of every piece
• Turn: the mirror turns after every move, so the twins change as you play
• Mirrorlock: every mirror and every idea at once

360 LEVELS, A STEADY CLIMB
Every chapter has 40 levels that start gentle and grow to an extreme level 40. Every level is built backwards from an empty board, so it can always be cleared, and checked by a solver.

A NEW DAILY MIRROR EVERY DAY
The same puzzle for every player, made on your phone from the date. Keep a streak going for bonus coins.

CALM BY DESIGN
• No full-screen ads between levels
• Optional videos only when you choose them
• Three free hints a day that show a safe pair, plus Undo
• Satisfying zips, chimes and haptics, all made for this game
• Progress stays on your device, with no account to make

SHARE YOUR CLEARS
After a level, save or share a picture of the board with your stars and a QR code, and ask your friends: can you free every twin without a bump?
